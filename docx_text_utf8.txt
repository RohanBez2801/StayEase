PREFACE
Shipiki
 is the name I chose for this system. It is a web-based hotel booking platform where guests can search for hotels, see which rooms are free, make a booking, pay online and manage their reservation afterwards. Hotel staff get an admin dashboard where they can look after rooms, prices and bookings without having to rely on paper diaries.
1.  Description of the Problem
Booking with 
Shipiki
 is a small chain of guest houses. 
At the moment
 they only take bookings over the phone or when someone walks in. Each branch keeps its own handwritten diary, and the room prices sit on a printed card that only gets updated when someone remembers to reprint it.
This way of working causes real problems. Because the diaries are separate, two people can book the same room without knowing â€“ a double-booking. Guests who live far away cannot check if a room is free before they travel, so they sometimes arrive to find the place full. Adding up a bill by hand (nights, tax, any discount) takes time and the numbers sometimes come out wrong. Management never sees proper occupancy figures because the diaries are never turned into data. Cancellations and refunds are also hard to follow because there is no single record linking a guest, their payment and their stay.
In short, 
Booking with 
Shipiki
 needs a system that guests can use from anywhere to check availability and book rooms online, that stops double-booking, that works out the charges automatically, and that gives management clear, up-to-date reports.
2.  Specific Objectives
I set the following clear, testable objectives for Book
ing
 with 
Shipiki
:
Let a guest search for available rooms by city, check-in and check-out dates, and number of guests.
Stop double-booking by checking and locking room availability the moment a booking is confirmed.
Work out the total cost of a stay automatically, including the nightly rate, tax and any discount.
Let a guest pay online and receive an email confirmation with a unique booking reference.
Let a guest view, and where the cancellation policy allows, cancel or change an existing booking.
Give hotel staff a dashboard to add and edit rooms, update rates, and see a calendar of bookings.
Produce simple management reports showing occupancy, revenue and the most booked room types for a chosen period.
Keep all data secure â€“ guest passwords encrypted and admin functions only open to authorised staff.
3.  Description of Existing Solution
Right 
now
 Booking with

Shipiki
 uses three separate tools that were never meant to work together. Phone bookings are written straight into an A4 diary â€“ one page per day â€“ with the guestâ€™s name, phone number and room number. Walk-in guests are given a room from a wall chart in reception that is updated in pencil so changes can be rubbed out. Prices are kept on a laminated card at the front desk; the receptionist works out the bill on a pocket calculator.
At the end of each month the branch manager copies numbers from the diary into a spreadsheet by hand so head office can see how many rooms were sold. That spreadsheet is emailed once a month. There is no daily or live view of bookings across the different branches.
4.  Evaluation of Existing Solution
Strengths
Cheap to run â€“ no software licences or hosting costs.
Staff already know how it works; no computer training needed.
Still works when the internet or electricity is down.
Weaknesses
No shared picture of which rooms are free, so 
double-bookings
 happen.
Guests cannot check availability or book outside office hours.
Manual price calculations are slow and sometimes wrong.
Diary pages and pencil charts can be lost, damaged or changed with no record of who did it.
Monthly hand-made reports arrive too late for management to act on problems.
No automatic confirmation or receipt is sent, so arguments about what was agreed are common.
Overall
 the manual system is cheap and simple, but it does not work well beyond one small branch. Its weaknesses are exactly the ones that cause lost revenue, guest complaints and late reports. A computerised booking system is needed.

8.  Description of Method of Solution
Book with 
Shipiki
 is a three-tier web application. The presentation tier is a responsive website that guests use to search, book and manage stays, and that staff use to manage rooms and view reports. The logic tier is a set of server-side services (one per module) exposed through a REST API. The data tier is a relational database that stores users, hotels, room types, availability, bookings and payments.
Key algorithm â€“ preventing double-booking
When a guest confirms a 
booking
 the system does not just check availability and then save the booking as two separate steps. Another guest could book the same room in between. 
Instead
 the check and the reserve happen inside one database transaction: the system re-reads the available room count for the requested dates, and only if enough rooms remain does it reduce the count and insert the booking â€“ all before any other request can read or write that row. If the count would go below zero the transaction is rolled back and the guest sees a â€œno longer availableâ€ message instead of a false confirmation.
Key algorithm â€“ price calculation
Look up the nightly rate for each date in the stay from the Availability table (rates can change by date).
Add the nightly rates together to get the room subtotal.
Apply any discount that applies (for example length-of-stay or a promotional code).
Add tax at the rate set for the hotelâ€™s country.
Store the full breakdown with the booking so the guestâ€™s receipt always matches what they were charged.

18.  Method of Solution Related to Problem
Section 1 identified four root causes of the problem: no shared view of availability (causing double-bookings), no way for distant guests to check availability, slow and error-prone manual price calculation, and reporting that arrives too late to be useful.
The transactional availability-locking algorithm in Section 8 directly removes double-booking, because two requests can no longer both read the same â€œavailableâ€ state and both go ahead â€“ the database guarantees only one succeeds. Publishing the same availability data on a public website, rather than a private diary, solves the second cause by letting any guest with internet access check rooms before travelling. The automatic price-calculation algorithm removes the arithmetic that previously caused billing 
errors, and
 stores a full breakdown so guests and staff can always see how a total was reached. Finally, because every booking is now a row in a shared database rather than an entry in a paper diary, the Reporting Module can produce occupancy and revenue figures on demand instead of once a month, giving management the up-to-date view that was missing before.
In this way each part of the chosen method maps straight back to a specific weakness found in the analysis, rather than being a generic feature added for its own sake.
19.  Accurate Method of Solution
The finished system was checked against real, everyday booking scenarios to confirm that the method of solution produces accurate, trustworthy results and not just isolated test passes.
A stay that spanned a rate change (weekday rate then weekend rate) was booked. The stored breakdown correctly added the two different nightly rates rather than assuming one flat rate.
Two receptionist accounts tried to book the final room of a 
fully-booked
 date at almost the same moment. The transaction lock made sure exactly one booking was created and the room count never went negative.
A guest cancelled inside the free-cancellation window and was charged nothing. An identical booking cancelled after the window incurred the stored policyâ€™s penalty. This confirmed that the policy snapshot taken at booking time â€“ not the current policy â€“ governs each booking.
Monthly revenue calculated by the Reporting Module was cross-checked by manually adding up the 
total_amount

These checks show that the method of solution is not only correct in the isolated tests of Section 13, but also produces accurate results under the realistic, sometimes concurrent conditions the system will face in daily use at 
Booking with 
Shipiki
.
